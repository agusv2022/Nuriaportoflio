## Who is responsible for your data

This website is operated by Nuria Iglesias, an independent brand strategy
consultant. If you want to reach the data controller about anything on this
page, write to **hello@nuriaiglesias.com**.

## What we collect, and why

**The contact form.** When you fill it in we receive your name, your email
address and the message you wrote. We use those three things for one purpose
only: to reply to you. The legal basis is your consent, given by choosing to
send the form.

**Nothing else.** This site sets no cookies. There is no analytics, no tracking
pixel, no advertising script and no social media embed. Nothing is stored on
your device beyond what your browser caches to display the page.

Fonts are served from this domain, so loading the site does not send your IP
address to Google or to any other third party.

## Who else sees it

Messages sent through the form are processed by **Formspree** (Formspree Inc.,
United States), which forwards them to our inbox and stores them for 30 days.
Transfers outside the EEA are covered by the Standard Contractual Clauses.
See formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy for their terms.

The website is hosted by **Cloudflare**, which keeps standard server logs
including IP addresses for security and abuse prevention.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone for marketing.

## How long we keep it

Contact messages are kept for as long as the conversation is useful, and no
longer than two years from the last exchange. After that they are deleted.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct
it, delete it, restrict how we use it, or send it to you in a portable format.
You can also withdraw consent at any time. Write to
**hello@nuriaiglesias.com** and we will respond within one month.

If you believe your data has been mishandled you have the right to complain to
the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version appears on this page with a new
date. Material changes will be flagged at the top.
