## Where they were

Ultramar had spent four years building a swimwear line made in Alicante, in
small runs, with fabric that costs three times what the high street pays. Their
website said "sustainable swimwear for everyone". Everyone is not a customer.

The audience they had defined in 2021 no longer matched the one actually
buying. Sixty-one per cent of orders came from women over 35 buying a single
high-value piece, while all the communication was aimed at a twenty-something
who was never going to pay that price without a discount.

## What we did

We rebuilt the audience from transaction data instead of assumption, then ran a
benchmark against eleven European swimwear brands in the same price bracket to
find out what nobody was claiming.

The gap was geography. Every competitor was talking about sustainability;
none was talking about the Mediterranean as a place rather than a backdrop.

## Where it landed

Ultramar stopped being a sustainable swimwear brand and became a Mediterranean
one that happens to be made responsibly. Same product, same factory, different
reason to buy.

The discount calendar went from nine campaigns a year to three, and average
order value tripled over two seasons.
