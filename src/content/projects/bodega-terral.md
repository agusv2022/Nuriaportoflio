## Where they were

Terral makes about eleven thousand bottles a year from old vines outside
Requena. The wine wins tastings. It was sitting on shelves at fourteen euros,
between two industrial labels, and moving slowly.

Their read was that they needed more distribution. The market analysis said the
opposite.

## What we did

We mapped the price bands in Spanish specialist retail and found the fourteen
euro shelf is the worst place a small producer can be: too expensive to be an
everyday bottle, too cheap to be read as anything special.

The SWOT was uncomfortable to write. The biggest weakness was not the wine or
the label. It was that nothing about how it was sold gave anyone a reason to
pay more.

## Where it landed

Terral left the supermarket channel and moved into specialist retail at
twenty-six euros, with the vineyard age and the production run on the front
label instead of the back.

Nine stockists in the first year, and the same eleven thousand bottles now
clear before the next vintage.
